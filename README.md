# Sales Analysis & Dashboard Using Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, customer, product, regional, and shipping data using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, PivotTables, KPIs, and an interactive dashboard.

The final dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional sales, and delivery efficiency.

---

## Business Problem

The business has a large volume of sales, customer, product, and shipping data stored in csv format, making it difficult to quickly identify sales trends, high-performing products and customers, regional performance, and delivery efficiency.

The objective is to clean and analyze the sales data using Excel and create a clear sales analysis report that helps the business understand its overall performance and identify areas for improvement.

The analysis will focus on:

* Tracking total sales and order performance.
* Identifying monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Analyzing sales by category and sub-category.
* Identifying top-performing products and customers.
* Comparing sales across regions and customer segments.
* Analyzing customer value and total sales per customer.
* Evaluating delivery time and shipping modes.
* Identifying key trends and business insights from the data.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support better sales, customer, and operational decisions.

### Key Business Questions

* What are the total sales and order performance?
* How do sales change month-over-month and year-over-year?
* Which categories and sub-categories generate the most sales?
* Which products and customers contribute the most sales?
* Which regions and customer segments perform best?
* What is the sales contribution of individual customers?
* How long does it take to deliver orders?
* Which shipping modes are associated with different delivery times?
* What trends and patterns can be identified from the sales data?

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was prepared in Excel before performing the analysis.

### Cleaning Steps

* Removed duplicate records.
* Checked and handled missing values.
* Corrected date and numerical data types.
* Standardized categorical and text values.
* Removed unnecessary spaces from text fields.
* Checked numerical values for inconsistencies.
* Validated date-related fields.
* Created and validated calculated columns.
* Created month and year fields for trend analysis.
* Created customer-level sales calculations.
* Calculated delivery days.
* Checked formulas and data for errors.
